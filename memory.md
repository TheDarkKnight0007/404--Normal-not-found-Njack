# The Museum of Incorrect Physics

## Project Vision
An immersive interactive museum experience where the laws of physics have been made unnaturally perfect.
Visitors explore impossible phenomena and gradually discover that perfect order has removed unpredictability, creativity, variation, and human feeling.

## Core Question
"What do we lose when everything behaves exactly as expected?"

## Core Narrative Arc
**Perfect order became the problem.**
The institution sought to eliminate all friction, error, and deviation. But in doing so, it sterilized the human condition. Each exhibit demonstrates an impossible law that directly mirrors a human emotional crisis.

## Curatorial Exhibits & Human Themes

### EXHIBIT 000 · THE SPACETIME FABRIC (THE OPENING PROLOGUE)
- **Object:** A giant Einsteinian spacetime wireframe mesh suspended in darkness (75%–88% viewport).
- **Physical Distortion:**
  - Negative Gravity / Repulsion: Visitor's presence pushes reality away instead of attracting it.
  - Non-local causality: Perturbations manifest at distant mirrored coordinates.
  - Retrograde & Pre-cognitive waves: Inward ripples converging before pointer contact.
  - Premature recovery: Disobeying momentum conservation.
- **Continuous Transformation:** Mesh tears -> nodes lift upward under inverted gravity -> particles condense into philosophical text ("ORDER" collapses -> "certainty" retro-reflection -> "anomaly" singularity vortex -> "THEORY" accumulation and catastrophic failure -> Title Reveal -> Architecture).

### EXHIBIT 001 · GRAVITY
- **Title:** "The Weight of Invisible Things"
- **Human Theme:** Invisible burdens, expectations, societal pressure.
- **Physical Distortion:** Mass without physical gravity, where objects are weighed down not by mass, but by observation and unspoken expectations.

### EXHIBIT 002 · TIME
- **Title:** "The Pendulum That Never Arrives"
- **Human Theme:** Procrastination, perfectionism, moving goalposts.
- **Physical Distortion:** A trajectory calibrated with infinite precision that perpetually halves the distance to its apex, never completing the cycle, frozen in the anxiety of completion.

### EXHIBIT 003 · OPTICS
- **Title:** "The Room of Perfect Reflections"
- **Human Theme:** Identity, perception, social expectations, the mask.
- **Physical Distortion:** Mirrors that reflect not the optical photons of who you are, but the mathematically flawless, sterile projection demanded by the outside world.

### EXHIBIT 004 · SYMMETRY
- **Title:** "The Garden of Equal Shapes"
- **Human Theme:** Conformity, standardisation, the death of idiosyncrasy and creativity.
- **Physical Distortion:** Organic geometric forms forced into rigorous, flawless bilateral alignment where no leaf or flower is allowed an asymmetrical flaw.

## The Archival Revelation & Resolution
- **The Archive:** Unredacted curatorial confessions revealing that curators and scientists broke down under the weight of unyielding perfection.
- **Control Room & The Choice:**
  - **RESTORE THE LAWS:** Choose pristine, unblemished, lifeless stasis.
  - **RELEASE THE ANOMALIES:** Re-embrace human messiness, unpredictable drift, creative chaos, and the beauty of imperfection.

## Technical Constraints & Execution Philosophy
- Use ONLY: HTML, CSS, Vanilla JavaScript.
- Do NOT use: React, Vue, Angular, Tailwind, Bootstrap, Three.js, GSAP, jQuery, external animation libraries, external frameworks.
- Lightweight, hackathon-friendly architecture: Prioritize execution quality, emotional atmosphere, poetic copy, and visual storytelling over unnecessary engineering complexity.
